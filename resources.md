## 🛠️ Tools and Libraries

| Name | Link | Notes |
|:----|:----|:----|
| LangChain | [GitHub](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain) | LangChain is useful for building applications that leverage large language models by providing a framework to easily integrate external data, tools(web search,calculator...etc), and memory(vector database) for context-aware, scalable AI solutions. |
| CapCut | [Official Site](https://www.capcut.com) | CapCut is a free(latest versions are not 🥲), user-friendly video editing tool ideal for creating promotional content such as short ads, Reels, and educational videos. It offers built-in templates, AI text-to-speech, subtitles, and effects to produce professional-looking videos quickly.|
